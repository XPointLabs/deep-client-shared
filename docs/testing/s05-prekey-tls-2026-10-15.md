# S05 — publication renewal and signed-entry TLS

Date: 2026-10-15. Candidate, not full/source or installed acceptance.
Publication semantics belong to
[DR-0121](../../../docs/survival-program/decisions/DR-0121-prekey-publication-placement-lifecycle.md).

The typed signed-entry transport requires a full TLS handshake instead of session
resumption. It still independently requires the exact signed SPKI, certificate
name, validity, usage and current entry authority; no platform/global trust
change or CA-managed Registry pinning was introduced. Closed diagnostic failure
codes carry no peer identifiers or certificate/request bytes. Diagnostic SPKI
classification cannot change a rejected validation outcome by throwing.
